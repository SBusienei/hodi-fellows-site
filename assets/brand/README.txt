Google Forms: Customize theme > Header > Choose image > Upload.
Select form-header.png and keep the full banner in the crop. After upload,
keep the custom accent #2F6FDB and the Gray #F6F6F6 background (the closest
Google Forms preset to the website's page background). The banner provides
the exact sky-blue website header. Uploading a header does not publish a form.
Google Forms offers Nunito but not Lato: use Nunito for the form title and
Arial for questions/body. The banner itself uses the exact website fonts.

The original SVGs are editable. PNGs are ready to upload and have the typography
rendered in. SVG text uses Nunito and Lato with Arial fallbacks. The adjacent
font files and SIL Open Font License notices are supplied for editing.
The badge uses the exact door paths, saffron tile and ink colour from the
website navigation. The header/card reuse the website's original learning-path
illustration. Bundled SVG exports embed that illustration for portability.
Nunito is the wordmark font; Lato is used for headlines and supporting text,
matching the website. These are not employer logos.
No dates, application-opening claims, student data or contact details appear
in these reusable assets.
