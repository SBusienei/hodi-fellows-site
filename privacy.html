Privacy policy

Last updated 23 November 2026

This policy explains what the Hodi Fellows application collects, why, who can see it and how long it is kept.

Who is responsible

This notice concerns the Hodi Fellows application and public website.

Application data controller: Hodi Fellows Organization.

The controller is responsible for deciding how applications are used, restricting access and handling privacy requests. Contact us at any time about your information using the address above.

What an application asks for

First name, surname, email, university or college, course or programme, study year, coding experience, and agreement to the self-guided weekly commitment and application contact. Coding skills, languages and a GitHub profile, repository or GitHub Pages link are optional.

Do not include passwords, identity documents, student IDs, private repository contents or other people's personal information. The application does not request gender, health information or payment.

Applying is voluntary. Required questions are marked on the form; missing them prevents submission. Optional answers may be left blank. If you are under 18, contact the programme before submitting so that appropriate permission and safeguards can be arranged.

Why the information is needed

We use your information to review eligibility, coding background and availability, make human selection decisions, and contact applicants about selection and this intake. There is no automated admission score.

Application contact permission is not permission for marketing, unrelated research or a public fellow profile. A separate choice would be needed for those purposes. We process your application on the basis of your consent, given when you submit the form. You can withdraw it at any time and we will delete your application.

Google Forms and access to responses

The application button opens Google Forms. Google processes submissions under its service terms and privacy policy, including service information it may collect when you visit. Processing may take place outside Kenya, under Google's terms and safeguards.

Responses are held in the owner's Google Form and a private linked Google Sheet, available only to the organiser and any specifically authorised selection reviewers. Response summaries are turned off. Owner email notifications indicate a new response; they are not a promise to email the complete application.

Application answers are never published on this website, committed to GitHub or used to create a public student directory. Providing a public GitHub link does not give permission to publish the rest of an application.

Retention and deletion

Unsuccessful and waitlisted applications are deleted within 30 days after selection closes. Accepted applicants' application records are deleted within 30 days after Phase 1 ends. Any continued programme contact needs its own agreed purpose and retention period.

Deletion is carried out manually by the organiser in Google Forms, the linked Google Sheet and any downloaded copies. Google's own service and backup retention is governed by its policies; deletion cannot promise that every provider backup disappears immediately.

Security and your choices

Access to responses is restricted to the organiser and authorised reviewers, the Google account is protected with two-factor authentication, response links are never made public, and privacy requests and incidents are handled through the contact above.

Use the privacy contact to ask about your information, request access or correction, withdraw contact permission, object to processing or request deletion, subject to applicable legal requirements. Withdrawal may prevent further application or intake communication.

You can also seek guidance or raise a complaint with Kenya's Office of the Data Protection Commissioner. The Data Protection Act covers collection notices, rights, lawful processing, retention and safeguards, including requirements for children.

The public website

The current public pages have no analytics scripts and do not save application answers in browser storage. Following the application link contacts Google; simply reading these pages does not load a Google Form or Google Fonts.

The public deployment uses GitHub Pages. GitHub records visitors' IP addresses for security, whether or not they are signed into GitHub. See the GitHub Privacy Statement for its processing practices. The website does not use that information to grade or track applicants.

This notice does not describe an operational member-login service. Private curriculum access and any new collection require separate security and privacy arrangements before they are introduced.
