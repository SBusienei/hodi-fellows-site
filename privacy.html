Privacy policy

Last updated 25 November 2026

This explains what the Hodi Fellows application collects, why, who can see it, and your rights under Kenya's Data Protection Act, 2019.

Who we are

Hodi Fellows is run by Hodi Fellows Company, which is responsible for your application data.

What we collect

  • Your name and email address
  • Your university or college, course and year of study
  • Your coding background, languages and answers to the coding check
  • Your availability and why you want to join
  • Your gender (optional, used only to help balance each cohort and for anonymous statistics)
  • Optional: a GitHub link

Please don’t include passwords, ID documents or other people’s personal information. If you are under 18, contact us before applying.

Why we use it

To check eligibility, select fellows for the cohort and contact you about your application. Selection is done by people, not by an automated system. We don’t use your information for marketing or share it with anyone else.

Who can see it

Only the organiser and anyone helping with selection. Applications are collected with Google Forms and stored in a private Google Sheet, so Google processes them under its privacy policy, and they may be stored outside Kenya. We never publish application answers.

The fellows page

Fellows can choose to appear on our fellows page. Each fellow adds their own profile (name, cohort, photo, a short bio, languages, links and, at the end, a quote about the programme) and decides whether it is published. We remove a profile whenever its owner asks.

How long we keep it

Unsuccessful and waitlisted applications are deleted within 30 days after selection closes. Accepted applicants' application records are deleted within 30 days after Phase 1 ends. Any continued programme contact needs its own agreed purpose and retention period.

The organiser carries out deletion manually in Google Forms, the linked Google Sheet and any downloaded copies. Google's service and backup retention is governed by its policies.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete your information, or withdraw your consent at any time, by contacting us using the email above. You can also contact Kenya’s Office of the Data Protection Commissioner.

This website

This site has no analytics or tracking. It is hosted on GitHub Pages, which logs visitors’ IP addresses for security under the GitHub Privacy Statement.
