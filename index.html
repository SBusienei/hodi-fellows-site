How it works

Over four weeks, you complete weekly tasks, build and review code with your group, and share your work in public. Planned check-ins help you stay on track. Any continuation will be confirmed after review.

Who can apply

  • Currently enrolled at a university or college
  • You can write basic code: variables, loops, functions
  • A laptop and regular internet access

No work experience or top grades required.
